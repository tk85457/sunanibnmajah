## 🌟 About Sunan Ibn Majah

**Sunan Ibn Majah (سنن ابن ماجه)**, compiled by **Imam Ibn Majah (rahimahullah)**, is the sixth major Hadith collection in the Kutub al-Sittah. It is renowned for its logical chapter organization and unique Hadith additions.

This Flutter app delivers the complete **Sunan Ibn Majah** collection with Urdu and English translations, fast offline search, and elegant dark theme.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📜 **Complete Collection**: All books and chapters of Sunan Ibn Majah.
- 🌐 **Dual Translations**: Accurate **Urdu** (سنن ابن ماجه اردو) and **English** translations.
- ⚡ **Offline Search**: Search by Hadith number or search terms.
- 📡 **100% Offline**: Embedded database requiring no internet connection.
- 📌 **Bookmarks**: Save important Hadiths and study notes.
